Tessellator rejects a valid polygon whose holes touch the outer ring and each other at single vertices

Since 10.5.0, LatLonShape and XYShape throw "Unable to Tessellate shape" for a valid polygon whose holes touch the outer ring and each other at single corners: a test made inclusive by #15739 lets the ring be cut along a zero-length diagonal.

The bug in 30 seconds

#

To index a polygon, Lucene cuts it into triangles. It first joins the holes to the outer ring, then clips triangles (ears) off one by one; when no ear is left, it cuts the remaining ring in two along a diagonal and goes on with each piece.

  1. Index an L-shaped polygon, outer ring (0,0) (0,12) (4,12) (4,4) (12,4) (12,0), with three small triangular holes: two meet the outer ring at its inner corner V = (4,4), and the third touches one of them at (3,4). Every touch is a single shared corner, which the Tessellator javadoc allows.
  2. You expect XYShape.createIndexableFields (or LatLonShape) to return triangles covering the polygon: 10.4.0 returns 13 triangles with total area 80 - 4.5 = 75.5.
  3. Lucene joins the holes into one 17-vertex ring that passes through V three times, clips 4 triangles, gets stuck, and looks for a diagonal that cuts the rest in two. Since #15739 its corner test counts a point exactly on an edge as inside (<= 0 instead of < 0), so it accepts a cut of length zero between two copies of V.
  4. That cut leaves hole 0's outline, (1,2) (4,4) (2,1), as a loop of its own, wound the wrong way, which cannot be cut into triangles. Indexing throws IllegalArgumentException: Unable to Tessellate shape, and the document is not indexed.
  5. 810 of the 972 hole orders and ring start vertices of this polygon fail on main; none fail on 10.4.0, which gives every one of them the exact area.

Who is affected

#

Anyone indexing polygons with holes through LatLonShape or XYShape (triangle fields and shape doc values, default and strict path) on 10.5.0 to 10.5.2 or main. The known trigger: two holes sharing a reflex corner of the outer ring and a third hole chained to one of them, at exact shared vertices; rare. The document fails loudly; nothing wrong is indexed.

Mechanism, terms and numbers656 words

Since 10.5.0, LatLonShape and XYShape refuse to index a valid polygon whose holes touch the outer ring and each other only at single vertices: createIndexableFields throws IllegalArgumentException: Unable to Tessellate shape. Possible malformed shape detected. #15739 made the reflex-corner branch of Tessellator.isLocallyInside inclusive (<= 0) to pick the right hole bridge for #15731; the same function also guards where splitEarcut may cut a ring in two, and there the inclusive test accepts a cut of length zero between two copies of a vertex the holes share with the outer ring. One piece is then a hole's own outline, which cannot be triangulated. 10.4.0 tessellates the repro polygon exactly (13 triangles, area 75.5); 10.5.0 to 10.5.2 and main reject it (on main, 810 of its 972 hole orders and start vertices fail). The failure is loud: one document cannot be indexed, nothing wrong is indexed.

Terms

#
  • Hole merging. eliminateHoles joins each hole to the outer ring, either at a vertex they share or through a bridge, which leaves two copies of each bridge end in the ring. The result is one ring that can pass through the same point several times.
  • Ear clipping. earcutLinkedList cuts off convex corners (ears) as triangles. When none is left it filters points, cures small self-intersections and finally calls splitEarcut, which looks for a diagonal that cuts the ring in two, checked by isValidDiagonal.
  • isLocallyInside(a, b) answers "does b lie inside the corner of the ring at a?". For a reflex corner (more than 180 degrees) it accepts b when b is on the inner side of either edge at a. #15739 changed both tests from < 0 to <= 0 (Tessellator.java:1263-1266), so a b exactly on one of the edges now counts as inside.

The bug, on the repro polygon

#

Outer ring (0,0) (0,12) (4,12) (4,4) (12,4) (12,0); V = (4,4) is its reflex corner. Hole 0 (4,4) (1,2) (2,1) and hole 1 (3,4) (4,4) (3,5) touch the ring (and each other) only at V; hole 2 (2,6) (1,5) (3,4) touches hole 1 only at (3,4). Area 80 - 2.5 - 0.5 - 1.5 = 75.5.

  1. Hole merging gives a 17-vertex ring that passes through V three times: (1,2) (4,4) (4,12) (0,12) (0,0) (1,5) (2,6) (3,4) (3,5) (4,4) (3,4) (1,5) (0,0) (12,0) (12,4) (4,4) (2,1).
  2. Ear clipping cuts 4 triangles and is stuck with 13 vertices; it falls back to splitEarcut (Tessellator.java:681-689, :909-950).
  3. isValidDiagonal(a, b) (:1205-1229) is asked about a and b, two copies of V. Its first checks, isLocallyInside(a.previous, b) and isLocallyInside(b.next, a) (:1209-1210), look at the reflex corners (3,5) and (2,1). Because a and b are the same point, the tested point lies exactly on the corner's own edge to V, so one area is exactly 0: <= 0 passes both checks, < 0 fails both. For equal points the method then returns true at once (:1216-1217).
  4. The ring is cut at V into (3,5) (4,4) (2,1) (1,2) (4,4) (4,12) (2,6) (3,4) and (12,4) (3,4) (1,5) (0,0). The first piece clips 3 ears and is left with (1,2) (4,4) (2,1): hole 0's outline, wound the wrong way. It has no ear and no diagonal, so earcutLinkedList throws (:687-688).
  5. 10.4.0, with < 0, refuses that cut, cuts (1,2)-(1,5) instead and returns 13 triangles of total area 75.5.

Which call site matters

#

isLocallyInside is called seven times, in four places: the bridge search (fetchHoleBridge, :584), cureLocalIntersections (:875, :876), and isValidDiagonal's neighbour checks (:1209-1210) and main checks (:1219-1220). Running all 16 strict/inclusive combinations against the repro and upstream TestTessellator (70 tests run; its 6 @Nightly tests and 1 @AwaitsFix test are skipped): the repro fails exactly when the neighbour checks are inclusive, and testComplexPolygon64 (#15731) fails exactly when the bridge search is strict. The other two sites change neither result.

Exposure and likelihood355 words
  • Who is hit. Applications on 10.5.0, 10.5.1, 10.5.2 or main that index polygons with holes through LatLonShape or XYShape: createIndexableFields (run) and createDocValueField (read: the same Tessellator.tessellate call), with the default checkSelfIntersections=false and with true. No special configuration and no scale needed: the exception comes from building the fields, before any codec or IndexWriterConfig setting plays a part. Users upgrading from 10.4.x are exposed: 10.4.0 was cut from branch_10_4, which never got the change.
  • Effect. That document cannot be indexed: the exception comes while its fields are built, so the application never gets fields to hand to IndexWriter. The repro builds the fields only and opens no writer; by reading the code, nothing wrong is produced and nothing reaches the index, so other documents, queries and the index are not affected.
  • What it takes. The minimised repro needs two holes that share a reflex vertex of the outer ring, and a third hole that touches one of them at another vertex, all at exact shared vertices; this is one known trigger, not a proven necessary condition. The outcome depends on the hole order and the start vertex of each ring (810 of 972 combinations fail) and on the coordinates: of 8 scales x 5 real-world offsets, 15 of 40 LatLon and 17 of 40 XY variants fail on main and none of 80 on 10.4.0. 29 of the 32 failures are at scales 1, 0.5 and 0.25, where the scaled coordinates are exact binary fractions, 3 at 1e-3 or 1e-5, none at 0.1, 0.01 or 1e-4. That points to floating-point rounding, not lat/lon quantization: the Tessellator computes on the input coordinates, not the encoded ones (Tessellator.java:1665-1672). A tilted variant without collinear edges fails too.
  • How likely. Touching holes do occur in real data (upstream fixed #15554, #15695 and #15731 for valid touching-hole polygons in early 2026), but this chained layout is narrow, and no public report of it was found since 10.5.0 shipped (2026-06-25). In a sweep of 9,000 random valid lattice polygons with the other known Tessellator bugs fixed, 8 were still rejected, 2 of them for this reason.

Why it is a bug, not intended

#
  • The javadoc allows it ("Holes may only touch at one vertex"); JTS accepts the polygon, and every proper subset of its holes tessellates on main.
  • 10.4.0 tessellates it exactly (13 triangles, area 75.5); upstream tests require holes touching the shell and another hole to tessellate.
  • #15739 set out to fix a wrong hole bridge (#15731), not to reject other polygons.
The full argument345 words
  • Contract. The class javadoc (Tessellator.java:40-52, abridged) requires "valid polygons: No self intersections; Holes must be inside the polygon; Holes may only touch at one vertex; Polygon must have an area (e.g., no "line" boxes)", plus a note on overflow with tiny values. Every touch here is one shared vertex. JTS 1.20 IsValidOp accepts the polygon and all 8 hole subsets; an independent check with exact integer arithmetic agrees. XYShape/LatLonShape promise "indexable fields for polygon geometry", and the strict path rejects this valid polygon as well.
  • Correct before. 10.4.0 and 10.3.0 return 13 triangles, area 75.5, whose union equals the polygon. Every proper subset of the holes tessellates on main with the right area and full coverage on a sample grid.
  • Tests. TestTessellator requires polygons whose holes touch the rest of the polygon at two or more distinct vertices to tessellate with the area matching. In testComplexPolygon41 (:724), 57 (:897), 61 (:932) and 62 (:938) one hole touches both the shell and another hole at different vertices, as hole 1 does here; in 53 (:863), 54 (:869) and 55 (:875) 8 to 11 holes each touch other rings at two or more points. The area is compared with delta 0 for 41, 53-55 and 62, and 3e-11 and 1e-11 for 57 and 61 (:900, :935). The #15731 tests 64 and 65 are not examples: no hole in them touches other rings at two or more points.
  • History. Upstream fixed every earlier rejection of a valid touching-hole polygon as a bug: #13841, #15205, #15554, #15695, #15731. #15739's PR text and CHANGES entry only describe #15731. The PR text: "We were failing to detect this case and choosing an invalid bridge to eliminate the hole"; the CHANGES entry: "In this case we might fail to detect it and choose a wrong bridge to eliminate the hole" (lucene/CHANGES.txt:1174-1175); its one review is an LGTM.
  • The ported library. mapbox/earcut keeps the strict < 0 in locallyInside.
The devil's-advocate check227 words

The strongest case for "intended" (built by the intended-behaviour checker, the cross-model reviewer and the adjudication): read "Holes may only touch at one vertex" as one contact per hole (hole 1 touches at two); note that Tessellator is @lucene.internal (Tessellator.java:72), that Polygon is @lucene.experimental and warns that self-crossing input "may result in unexpected behavior" (Polygon.java:34, :41), and that the message says "Possible malformed shape"; add that three rings meet at V, and call the collinear edges degenerate input. JTS validity is an outside standard and does not settle how maintainers read the one-vertex rule.

Why it fails: the two-hole control {hole 1, hole 2} has the same two contacts on hole 1 and passes on main; upstream's own tests require holes that touch at two or more distinct vertices to tessellate (41, 53-55, 57, 61, 62); the polygon is not self-crossing; the public XYShape and LatLonShape methods carry no internal tag; a tilted variant with no collinear hole and shell edges still fails; and 10.4.0 returns an exact tessellation, so 10.5 replaced a correct result with a rejection. The cross-model fact check of this doc rated the case moderate (the literal precondition, the two tags, three rings at V) and still found the evidence against it more persuasive; the earlier reviewers rated it weak or rejected it. Verdict: not intended, a regression.

Downstream note

#

Downstream: an application that indexes user-supplied polygons may see such a document refused with an IllegalArgumentException; nothing else in the index is affected.

How it was found

#

Translation validation: a harness tessellated 9,000 valid polygons with up to four holes on a 13 x 13 grid, many with touching holes, and checked each result (no rejection, no overlaps, exact area). With the other known Tessellator bugs fixed, 8 were still rejected; 2 of them pass with #15739's comparison reverted, and dropping holes from one gave this polygon.

The method in full235 words

Method: translation validation (semi-formal): the stock Tessellator's output on generated inputs is checked against an exact oracle.

  • A harness generated JTS-valid polygons with 0-4 holes on a 13 x 13 lattice (3 seeds x 3,000 XY polygons; one seed also with checkSelfIntersections=true and, for its first 1,500 polygons, as lat/lon), many of them with holes touching the shell or each other at a vertex. For each it checked: no rejection; every triangle non-degenerate and inside the polygon; triangles pairwise interior-disjoint (both with JTS); total area exactly equal (doubled integer areas); edge flags equal to "this edge lies on a ring".
  • On stock main most failures belong to already known Tessellator bugs. With their fixes and two sibling candidates' fixes applied, 8 rejections remained: 5 with a hole vertex in the middle of another ring's edge (filed with the known LFV-210 family) and 3 with only vertex touches. Two of those (poly#389, poly#2114) pass with #15739's comparison reverted; the third (poly#2529) does not, so it has another cause, not analysed here.
  • Shadowing the versions of Tessellator.java before and after #15739 onto the main jars (an A/B of the two adjacent file versions, not a git bisect) pinned it to #15739; reverting its two hunks separately showed that the comparison change causes it. Dropping holes from poly#2114 gave the three-hole repro.
  • This doc's work then localised the call site (16 combinations) and traced the zero-length cut.

Bug record

#

LFV-755, snapshot of 2026-10-30

Registry title
Tessellator rejects a valid polygon whose holes touch the outer ring and each other at single vertices with 'Unable to Tessellate shape', because #15739 made the reflex-vertex test in isLocallyInside inclusive ('<= 0'): a regression in 10.5.0 (10.4.0 accepts the polygon)
Area
geo / Tessellator hole elimination (polygon indexing for LatLonShape and XYShape)
Priority
Low, rank 355: A 10.5.0 regression that loudly rejects a valid polygon with chained vertex-touching holes, a crash with a clear exception for a narrow layout like LFV-762, so it closes the Tessellator group right after that row.
Impact class
crash
Impact note
Hits applications on 10.5.0-10.5.2 or main that index polygons with holes through the default LatLonShape or XYShape createIndexableFields or createDocValueField (default codec, IndexWriterConfig and checkSelfIntersections setting; the strict path rejects it too; no scale needed) when two holes share a reflex vertex of the outer ring and a third hole touches one of them at another vertex, all at exact shared vertices: the valid polygon (JTS IsValidOp accepts it, and 10.4.0 tessellates it exactly) is rejected with IllegalArgumentException 'Unable to Tessellate shape. Possible malformed shape detected.' while its fields are built, so that one document cannot be indexed; the failure is loud (no wrong triangles are indexed, no other document, query or index state is affected), depends on hole order and vertex rotation (810 of 972 orderings of the repro are rejected on main, 0 on 10.4.0) and on coordinate scale after lat/lon quantization, and how often real data has this layout was not measured.
Summary
You index a valid polygon with holes, for example an L-shaped outer ring (0,0) (0,12) (4,12) (4,4) (12,4) (12,0) with three small triangular holes that touch the ring and each other only at single shared corners, which the Tessellator javadoc allows ('Holes may only touch at one vertex'), and you expect XYShape.createIndexableFields or LatLonShape.createIndexableFields to return its triangles, as 10.4.0 does. Instead, since #15739 (first released in 10.5.0), both calls throw IllegalArgumentException 'Unable to Tessellate shape. Possible malformed shape detected.', so the document cannot be indexed, although every proper subset of the holes tessellates. The cause is that #15739 changed the reflex-vertex branch of Tessellator.isLocallyInside from '< 0' to '<= 0', which accepts a candidate exactly collinear with a neighbouring edge; reverting that comparison fixes this polygon but breaks the #15731 polygon #15739 was written for, so a correct fix has to be more selective.
