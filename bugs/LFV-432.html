RandomSamplingFacetsCollector never samples the first hit of a segment that starts mid-bin

RandomSamplingFacetsCollector keeps one random hit from each run of hits, but when the chosen hit is the next segment's first hit, that run gets no sample. 4 hits in segments [1 | 3], sample size 2: 1 sampled hit instead of 2; one > to >= fixes it.

The finding in 30 seconds

#

Sampling keeps one hit per bin. A bin that a segment boundary cuts in two is meant to continue in the next segment; when its chosen hit is that segment's first hit, the carry-over is not recorded and the bin loses its sample.

Background

#
  1. Facet sampling. RandomSamplingFacetsCollector (lucene/facet, opt-in) counts facets on a sample of the hits when a query has more than sampleSize hits. amortizeFacetCounts divides the sampled counts by the sampling rate, sampleSize / totalHits, to estimate the full counts.
  2. One hit per bin. It walks the hits in doc order across segments, cuts them into bins of binSize = totalHits / sampleSize (rounded down) hits, and keeps the hit at a random offset in each bin.
  3. Carry-over. Each segment is sampled on its own. When a segment ends inside a bin, two fields carry the rest of the bin into the next segment: how many of its hits are still to come (leftoverBin) and the offset of the hit to sample there (leftoverIndex, or -1 if the bin was already sampled).

The finding, step by step

#
  1. Index 4 documents in two segments, 1 then 3 (NoMergePolicy, a commit after the first). Run a match-all query through RandomSamplingFacetsCollector.createManager(2, 1): sample size 2, sampler seed 1.
  2. 4 hits and sample size 2 give a sampling rate of 0.5. The collector cuts the hits, in order across segments, into runs ("bins") of 2, {hit 0, hit 1} and {hit 2, hit 3}, and keeps one random hit from each. You expect 2 sampled hits.
  3. Seed 1 picks the second hit of the first bin, hit 1, which is the first hit of segment 2. You get 1 sampled hit: the first bin contributes nothing, and for a label on all 4 documents amortizeFacetCounts reports 2 instead of 4.
  4. Why: when a segment ends inside a bin, the code carries the choice over only if it lies beyond the hits seen (randomIndex > counter), or marks the bin done if already sampled (<). The equal case, the next segment's first hit, matches neither branch, so that hit is never sampled.
  5. Nothing fails. On average the sample shrinks by at most about (segment boundaries) / (hits): a quarter here, where half the seeds lose half the sample, and about 0.05% at 19,940 hits over 20 segments. Changing > to >= fixes it.

Who is affected

#

Applications that opt in to facet sampling with RandomSamplingFacetsCollector, on multi-segment indexes, for queries with at least twice sampleSize hits. Each segment boundary inside a bin loses that bin's sample with probability about 1/binSize, so counts are on average low by at most about (boundaries) / (hits): large for few hits over many segments, about 0.05% at 19,940 hits.

Mechanism, terms and numbers781 words

RandomSamplingFacetsCollector (lucene/facet, opt-in) estimates facet counts from a sample: when a query has more than sampleSize hits, it cuts the hits, in doc order across segments, into bins of binSize = (int) (1.0 / samplingRate) hits and keeps the hit at a random offset in each bin; amortizeFacetCounts divides the sampled counts by samplingRate = sampleSize / totalHits. A bin that a segment boundary cuts in two is carried into the next segment through leftoverBin and leftoverIndex. At the end of a segment createSample handles only randomIndex > counter and randomIndex < counter; when the chosen hit is the first hit of the next segment (randomIndex == counter) neither branch runs, so the carried bin gets no sample, or one at a stale offset. The first hit of a segment that starts mid-bin is therefore never sampled, the sample is smaller than getSamplingRate() says, and amortized counts come out low, silently, in every release since 4.8.0. On average the sample shrinks by at most about (segment boundaries) / (hits): for 4 hits in segments [1 | 3] half the seeds sample 1 hit instead of 2, at 19,940 hits over 20 segments the loss is about 0.05%. The fix is > to >=.

The code (main 645d4fe14a20)

#
  1. Sampling starts in getMatchingDocs() when totalHits > sampleSize (RandomSamplingFacetsCollector.java:132-134): samplingRate = (1.0 * sampleSize) / totalHits (:137); createSample then runs once per segment, in order (:149-155), with binSize = (int) (1.0 / samplingRate) (:164).
  2. A segment's first bin (:169-177): if a bin was carried over, limit = leftoverBin and randomIndex = leftoverIndex ("either NOT_CALCULATED, which means we already sampled from that bin, or the next document to sample"); otherwise limit = binSize and randomIndex = random.nextInt(binSize).
  3. The loop (:179-189): the hit at counter == randomIndex is sampled (:180-181, the only sampleDocs.set); when counter reaches limit a new bin starts with a fresh randomIndex.
  4. End of segment (:191-207): counter == 0 clears the carry-over (:196): the segment ended on a bin edge, or had no hits. A segment with no hits still gets its own MatchingDocs, so it ends an outstanding carry-over (the loss the comment at :191-195 accepts) and the next segment starts a fresh bin, not mid-bin. Otherwise leftoverBin = limit - counter (:198), then if (randomIndex > counter) (:199, "the document to sample is in the next bin") sets leftoverIndex = randomIndex - counter (:201), and else if (randomIndex < counter) (:202) sets NOT_CALCULATED (:205). With randomIndex == counter the hit to sample is offset 0 of the next segment, but neither branch runs.
  5. What is left in leftoverIndex. Usually -1 (its initial value, :83, or cleared at :196 / :205): the carried bin starts with randomIndex = -1 and gets no sample. If an earlier carry left an offset k >= 1 that nothing cleared, the carried bin samples offset k instead of 0, or nothing when k is past the bin's end (follows from the code; not isolated in a test). A bin is never sampled twice.
  6. Consequence. leftoverIndex is only ever -1 or >= 1 (:83, :196, :201, :205), and a segment that starts mid-bin starts its loop with counter = 0, so its first hit is never sampled.

The numbers

#

In the repro, XORShift64Random(1).randomLong() is 0x2200011 (odd), so the first nextInt(2) is 1 (:55-71). Segment 1 (1 hit): limit 2, randomIndex 1; counter ends at 1, equal to randomIndex, so leftoverBin = 1 and leftoverIndex stays -1. Segment 2 (3 hits): the carried bin has limit 1 and randomIndex -1, so hit 1 is skipped; hits 2 and 3 form a fresh bin and one of them is sampled. Total 1; with >= it is 2.

A boundary inside a bin hits the equal case with probability about 1/binSize, so a query with B such boundaries loses up to about B/binSize of its about totalHits/binSize sampled hits: at most about B/totalHits. Executed: a bounded-exhaustive check on the stock jar (3-12 hits, sampleSize 2-3, every split into 1-3 segments, 50 seeds) left 8,429 of 76,250 complete bins without a sample and none with two, 0 with the fix. At 20 segments of 997 hits (sampleSize 200, binSize 99, 3,000 seeds) the first hit of a segment that starts mid-bin was sampled 0 of 57,000 times, 577 with the fix (about 576 expected); the mean sample was 201.285 against 201.393.

Exposure and likelihood232 words
  • Who. Only applications that opt in to facet sampling with RandomSamplingFacetsCollector (its constructors, or createManager(sampleSize, seed) with IndexSearcher.search). A read-only code search (2026-10-26) found no use of the class in Elasticsearch, OpenSearch or Solr.
  • When. A multi-segment index and a query with totalHits >= 2 * sampleSize, so that binSize >= 2; with binSize 1 every hit is sampled and no bin crosses a boundary. Each segment boundary that falls inside a bin then triggers it with probability about 1/binSize, as long as the next segment has hits (a segment with no hits drops the carried bin anyway). Deterministic for a given seed and segment layout, with assertions on and off.
  • What the user sees. Silently smaller samples than getSamplingRate() implies and amortized counts biased low. The first hit of every segment that starts mid-bin has sampling probability exactly 0: a systematic bias, not sampling noise.
  • How much. Large only for few hits over many segments: in the repro half the seeds sample 1 hit instead of 2 and the mean amortized count is 3.0 instead of 4; in small filtered multi-segment probe indexes 66 of 2,400 searches got an empty sample and so no facet result, 0 with the fix. Negligible at the scale the feature targets (CHANGES.txt: "in cases where there are millions of hits"): about 0.05% of the sample at 19,940 hits over 20 segments.

Why it looks unintended

#
  • The carry-over exists for this case: its comment reads "the document to sample is in the next bin".
  • The javadoc promises "a sample ratio of sampling size / totalN", which amortizeFacetCounts divides by; the real ratio is lower.
  • The only loss the code accepts is after a segment with no hits; here the segment has hits.
The full argument236 words
  • The comments. The branch that misses the case reads "the document to sample is in the next bin" (RandomSamplingFacetsCollector.java:200); the read-back says leftoverIndex is "either NOT_CALCULATED, which means we already sampled from that bin, or the next document to sample" (:171-172). In the equal case neither holds: the bin was not sampled.
  • The contract. The sampleSize javadoc: "sampling will be done using a sample ratio of sampling size / totalN" (:97-99); getSamplingRate() "Returns the sampling rate that was used" (:256-259), and amortizeFacetCounts divides by it (:240, :249).
  • Design history (#6539, LUCENE-5476). An early patch's notes (Rob Audenaerde) listed that in a small segment "there might be a few hits that are never touched", and Michael McCandless replied "I think these limitations are acceptable?". Shai Erera then noted that createSample "doesn't carry over the bin residue to the next segment ... it might affect the total sample size", and their last patch revision before the commit "Fixed a bug in how carryOver was implemented – replaced by two members leftoverBin and leftoverIndex", asking for "another look, to make sure this time leftover works well". The equal case was never discussed.
  • Tests. The only upstream test, TestRandomSamplingFacetsCollector, checks a chi-square bound on per-segment sample proportions and amortized counts within 1.0 of the expected value. Both tolerate this loss; it passes with and without the fix.
The devil's-advocate check208 words

The strongest case for "intended" (rated moderate by the cross-model reviews): sampleSize is only "The preferred sample size" (:97); getMatchingDocs warns that per-segment MatchingDocs "might be empty (not containing any hits) as result of sampling" (:115-116); the comment at :191-195 accepts dropping the carry-over after a segment with no hits ("complicates the code and doesn't seem so important"); and the 2014 thread called small-segment losses acceptable.

Why it fails: that accepted loss is scoped to the counter == 0 branch, whose comment covers a segment that ends exactly on a bin edge (nothing is lost) and a segment with no hits (the only case that drops an outstanding carry-over); here the segment has hits and the else branch computes the carry-over with the wrong comparison. The "acceptable" remark concerned an early patch with no carry-over, which was then added to fix it. The bug is not limited to tiny segments: any boundary inside a bin hits it with probability 1/binSize. Giving a reachable hit sampling probability 0, or reusing a stale offset from an earlier bin, is not a trade-off, and the fix costs nothing. Verdict: not intended, a defect (the independent reproduction, the cross-model review, the intended-behaviour check and the adjudication).

Trade-offs

  • Cost. None: one comparison per segment.
  • Behaviour change. For a fixed seed, which hits are sampled changes whenever the equal case occurs: the bin that lost its sample gets one again, so the sample and amortized counts rise. Tests or applications that pin a seed and expect exact sampled counts would see different numbers. Worth a CHANGES.txt entry under Bug Fixes.
  • Scope. A closes only the equal case. The binSize truncation (LFV-437, so the sample can still exceed sampleSize: mean 201.393 for sampleSize 200 in the scale check with the fix), the documented drop of a carried bin after a segment with no hits (:191-196) and the multi-slice reduce (LFV-765) are unchanged.
  • Neighbours. Open PR #16847 edits createSample (maxDoc() to maxLeafDoc()), so a fix may need a rebase; LFV-437, LFV-425 and LFV-765 in the same collector have their own fix sites.

Compute leftoverIndex in one assignment (equivalent cleanup, not run)

Trade-offs

Document the loss (not recommended)

#

Trade-offs

Keeps a reachable hit at sampling probability 0 and a sampling rate that overstates the real one, for no saving: the fix is one character.

Downstream note

#

An application that shows sampled facet counts gets slightly low numbers (no facet result for some very small hit sets); every sampled hit is still one of the query's own live, filtered hits.

How it was found

#

A hunt unit read createSample while inventorying the facet module's counts: the > / < pair leaves the equal case unhandled. A bounded-exhaustive check on the stock jar (3-12 hits, every split into 1-3 segments, 50 seeds) found 8,429 of 76,250 complete bins without a sample, 0 with the fix; a public-API JUnit confirmed it.

The method in full127 words

Method: non-formal (the hunt's tier for findings from code reading and directed testing).

Impact note
Only applications that opt in to facet sampling with RandomSamplingFacetsCollector are hit, on any multi-segment index once a query has about twice sampleSize hits or more (binSize >= 2): each segment boundary that falls inside a bin loses that bin's sample with probability about 1/binSize, so the sample is smaller than getSamplingRate() implies and amortized counts come out low by at most about (#segment boundaries)/totalHits; that is large only for few hits over many segments (4 hits in segments [1 | 3], sampleSize 2: half the seeds sample 1 hit instead of 2, mean amortized count 3.0 instead of 4; in small filtered probe indexes 66 of 2,400 searches got an empty sample and no facet result, 0 with the fix) and negligible at the scale the feature targets (about 0.05% of the sample at 19,940 hits over 20 segments); deterministic, -ea and -da, every release since 4.8.0.
Summary
You count facets with RandomSamplingFacetsCollector on a multi-segment index for a query with many more hits than sampleSize, and expect systematic sampling: one random hit out of every run of binSize consecutive hits, with a run that is cut by a segment boundary continued in the next segment, so every hit has about a 1/binSize chance to be sampled. Instead, when the hit picked for such a run is the first hit of the next segment, createSample leaves its carry-over state unset (it tests only randomIndex > counter and randomIndex < counter), so that run contributes no sample, or one at a stale offset, and the first hit of a segment that starts mid-bin is never sampled. Nothing fails; the sample and the amortized counts are just smaller than the reported sampling rate implies (4 hits in segments [1 | 3], sampleSize 2: half the seeds sample 1 hit instead of 2).
