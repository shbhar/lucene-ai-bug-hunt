TemporalMergePolicy.forceMergeDeletes merges segments older than setMaxAgeSeconds, across time windows

With TemporalMergePolicy and its opt-in setMaxAgeSeconds age limit, IndexWriter.forceMergeDeletes() merges over-age segments from different time windows into one, although the javadoc says such segments are never merged. In the repro a 2020 segment and a 2021 segment become one; forceMerge(1) keeps them apart.

The finding in 30 seconds

#

Each segment belongs to a time window; segments past the age limit share one "old data" bucket that is supposed to be left alone. One of the policy's three merge finders forgets to skip it.

Background: how TemporalMergePolicy groups segments

#
  1. Each segment is assigned to a time window by its newest timestamp. Recent windows are small (1 hour by default) and grow for older data, up to 365 days. Segments merge only with segments of the same window.
  2. With setMaxAgeSeconds(limit) (off by default), a segment whose newest timestamp is older than the limit gets the special bucket key -1, "old data", whatever window it came from. Such segments are meant never to be merged.
  3. The policy has three merge finders: natural merges (findMerges), forceMerge(n) and forceMergeDeletes(). The first two skip key -1.

The finding, step by step

#
  1. You index time-series data with TemporalMergePolicy on a LongPoint field ts and call setMaxAgeSeconds(30 days), so segments whose newest timestamp is older than 30 days count as cold data that is never merged.
  2. You commit two segments of 2 documents each: one stamped 2020-01-01, one 2021-01-01. Both are years past the limit and lie in different time windows.
  3. You delete one document in each segment (50%, above the default 10% threshold) and call forceMergeDeletes(). You expect 2 segments: the javadoc says over-age segments "will not be merged" and windows are "never merged together", and forceMerge(1) on the same input does keep both.
  4. Instead you get 1 segment holding the 2020 and the 2021 document. Every over-age segment shares one "old data" bucket, key -1. Natural merges and forceMerge skip that bucket; findForcedDeletesMerges merges it like any window, in batches of up to 8 whenever two or more qualify.
  5. Without the age limit the same call merges nothing: the setting meant to avoid cold-data I/O is what makes this call rewrite cold data. In the repro, hit counts and live documents are unchanged.

Who is affected

#

Only applications that choose the opt-in, experimental TemporalMergePolicy (since 10.5.0), turn on setMaxAgeSeconds (off by default) and call forceMergeDeletes() while two or more over-age segments carry over 10% deletions, e.g. after deleting or updating old documents. They get extra I/O on cold data and segments spanning several windows; in the repro, hit counts are unchanged.

Mechanism, terms and numbers527 words

TemporalMergePolicy puts each segment into a time window by its newest timestamp and promises two things: segments from different windows are never merged together, and, once you set an age limit with setMaxAgeSeconds, segments older than the limit are not merged at all, to save I/O on cold data. Natural merges and forceMerge(n) keep both promises. IndexWriter.forceMergeDeletes() does not: every over-age segment gets the same bucket key, -1, and findForcedDeletesMerges merges that bucket like an ordinary window, so a 2020 segment and a 2021 segment that each carry deletions become one segment. Without the age limit the same call leaves them apart. In the repro, hit counts and live documents are unchanged and the deletions are expunged; the cost is merge I/O on cold data and segments that span windows.

Mechanism (main 5adff78bb168, TemporalMergePolicy.java)

#
  1. assignToBucket keys a segment by its newest timestamp (:777-778). getBucketForTimestamp returns -1 when the segment is older than maxAgeSeconds: "If data is older than maxAgeSeconds, put it all in one monster \"old data\" bucket" (:790-794). So key -1 means "skip me", not "these belong together": it holds over-age segments from every window.
  2. findMergeCandidates (natural merges, :828-831) and findForcedMerges (forceMerge, :502-505) skip key -1 with the comment "Skip old data bucket (-1 sentinel) - don't merge very old data".
  3. findForcedDeletesMerges (:528-597) keeps the segments that are not already merging, have deletions, a deleted share strictly above forceMergeDeletesPctAllowed (default 10%, :111) and a readable ts point range (:544-562), groups them with the same groupSegmentsByTimeWindow (:568) and loops over every bucket (:571-594) with one guard, bucketSegments.size() < 2 (:573). Key -1 passes it, and buildSequentialMerges (:582, :951-978) merges its segments in batches of up to maxThreshold (default 8, :108), emitting a last batch only if it holds two or more (:970): one segment left over after full batches (the 9th of 9) keeps its deletions.
  4. Without an age limit, the 2020 and 2021 segments of the repro fall into different 365-day windows (starting 1,576,800,000 and 1,608,336,000 seconds, :796-812), each alone, and the size() < 2 guard leaves both. With the limit they meet in bucket -1 and are merged.

Exposure and likelihood147 words
  • Who is hit. TemporalMergePolicy is opt-in and @lucene.experimental (:94), first released in 10.5.0. setMaxAgeSeconds defaults to Long.MAX_VALUE, no limit (:106-107). The trigger is an explicit IndexWriter.forceMergeDeletes() call while at least two over-age segments have more deletions than forceMergeDeletesPctAllowed (default 10%, :111).
  • What changes. Qualifying over-age segments from any number of windows are rewritten together, in batches of up to 8 (maxThreshold, :108), with assertions on or off: extra I/O on exactly the data the age limit is for, and merged segments that span several windows, so a point-range query on ts can skip fewer segments by their min/max (search/PointRangeQuery.java:263-274).
  • What does not. No exception; in the repro the deletions are expunged and live documents and hit counts are unchanged (value probe). Natural merges, forceMerge(n) and forceMergeDeletes() without an age limit are not affected by this bug.

Why it looks unintended

#
  • The javadoc says over-age segments "are not merged" and windows are "never merged together", with no exception for forced deletes.
  • The other finders, from the same commit, skip the old-data bucket ("don't merge very old data"); an upstream test asserts forceMerge(1) leaves over-age segments alone.
  • The introducing PR lists "Old data protection" as a key constraint.
The full argument196 words
  • The class's own words. "segments from different time windows are never merged together" (TemporalMergePolicy.java:43-45); "Segments from different time windows are never merged together, even during IndexWriter#forceMerge(int)" (:87-89); "Very old segments (older than setMaxAgeSeconds) are not merged to avoid unnecessary I/O on cold data" (:91-92); setMaxAgeSeconds: such segments "will not be merged" and are "placed in a special \"old data\" bucket and skipped during merge selection" (:325-334).
  • The merge choice is the policy's. IndexWriter.forceMergeDeletes: "The actual merges to be executed are determined by the MergePolicy" (IndexWriter.java:2409-2410).
  • The siblings. findForcedMerges (:502-505) and findMergeCandidates (:828-831) skip key -1, from the same commit; the PR's first commit already lacked the skip only in findForcedDeletesMerges.
  • Tests. TestTemporalMergePolicyAcrossBuckets.testOldDataBucketWithMultipleOldSegments asserts "Old data segments (all >3 years) should NOT be merged (skipped)" after forceMerge(1) (:305-306). The forced-deletes tests (TestTemporalMergePolicy.java:121, :200) never set an age limit, so no test pins the current behaviour.
  • The PR. apache/lucene#15620 lists "Old data protection: Very old segments (configurable via maxAgeSeconds) are excluded from merging" under "Key Constraints" and says findForcedDeletesMerges "merges those within the same time window".
The devil's-advocate check195 words

The case for intended. (A) All over-age data is one bucket, so merging inside it is a merge within a bucket. (B) forceMergeDeletes is an explicit, costly request to "expunge all deletes" (MergePolicy.java:643-645), and on #15620 a reviewer (benwtrent, in a PR comment) wrote that they would expect merging with only expunging deletes to work as normal. An independent reviewer from another model family (OpenAI GPT-5.6) rated this case moderate.

Why it fails. The -1 key is a skip marker, as both sibling finders and the setMaxAgeSeconds javadoc treat it, not a time window; the explicit forceMerge(1) skips it too. The code does not implement "reclaim old deletions" either: a lone over-age segment with deletions is left alone, so old deletions are expunged only when two or more old segments qualify, and then by mixing windows. A reclaim design would rewrite each eligible old segment on its own (fix B). The reviewer's remark is about deletes in general, not the age limit. Whether forced deletes should be an exception is still the maintainers' call; if so, the remedy is fix B or a javadoc note, and the minimal test holds under either fix.

Suggested fix

#

Recommended Skip the -1 bucket, as findMerges and forceMerge do (validated)

Skip the old-data bucket in findForcedDeletesMerges, as the other finders do: if (entry.getKey() == -1) continue; at the top of the bucket loop. With it the repro and both upstream TemporalMergePolicy test classes pass (assertions on and off), as do 98 merge-policy tests and the core format, ECJ and forbidden-apis checks.

Trade-offs

  • Matches the documented contract and the sibling finders; no new behaviour.
  • Deletions in over-age segments are then never expunged by this policy, as with forceMerge today. A sentence in the setMaxAgeSeconds javadoc saying so would help.

Downstream note

#

Applications that rely on over-age segments never being rewritten (for example to keep them on cheaper storage) should avoid forceMergeDeletes() with an age limit until this is fixed.

How it was found

#

A semi-formal proof-obligation audit of TemporalMergePolicy's contract: no merge chosen by any of its find methods may include a segment older than the age limit or mix time windows. findForcedDeletesMerges lacks the old-data skip its two siblings have, and an executable test on main showed the merge. Independent reviewers re-ran it and argued the case for intended behaviour.

The method in full86 words
  • Method: the obligation "no chosen merge contains a segment in bucket -1 or segments of two windows" was checked against each of the policy's finders at 5adff78bb168. Natural merges (:828-831) and forceMerge (:502-505) discharge it by skipping key -1; findForcedDeletesMerges (:571-594) does not. An executable JUnit test on main confirmed it (2026-11-04).
  • Reviews: an independent reproduction with controls, an adversarial review by a model from another family (OpenAI GPT-5.6), an intended-behaviour review and a final review; see Independent checks.
