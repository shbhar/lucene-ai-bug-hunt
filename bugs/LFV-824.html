The classic Highlighter throws IllegalStateException for FieldExistsQuery when it highlights from term vectors

Since 10.5.0 the classic Highlighter throws IllegalStateException instead of highlighting when the query contains a FieldExistsQuery, or a full-range numeric query that rewrites to one, and the token stream comes from term vectors with positions and offsets. 10.4.0 highlights the same hit; restoring a skip that 10.5.0 removed fixes it.

The bug in 30 seconds

#

The classic Highlighter rewrites parts of your query against a tiny index of the one document it highlights. Since 10.5.0 that breaks for exists clauses when the tiny index is built from term vectors.

  1. You index one document: body = "the quick brown fox", stored with term vectors (positions and offsets), plus a doc-values field price = 3. The query +body:fox #FieldExistsQuery(price) finds it: 1 hit.
  2. You highlight the hit from its term vectors (TokenSources.getTermVectorTokenStreamOrNull) with new Highlighter(new QueryScorer(query, "body")). You expect the quick brown <B>fox</B>, which 10.4.0 returns.
  3. To find the words to mark, the highlighter rewrites each clause it has no special case for against a tiny one-field index built from the term vectors. Since 10.5.0 it no longer skips the exists clause, and that index describes every field, price included, as its own field shadowed_field: no norms, no doc values.
  4. So FieldExistsQuery rejects the field and getBestFragment throws IllegalStateException ("... field 'shadowed_field' exists and indexes neither of these data structures"). Every hit fails, and so does the full-range LongField.newRangeQuery("size", Long.MIN_VALUE, Long.MAX_VALUE), which rewrites to the same query.
  5. Re-analysing the text instead of reading term vectors still works: there the tiny index's field has norms, so the exists clause becomes match-all and is ignored.

Who is affected

#

Applications on 10.5.0 or later that highlight with the classic Highlighter and QueryScorer from term vectors with positions and offsets, when the highlighted query has an exists clause or a full-range numeric query on any field. Every hit fails. Search results, re-analysed text, offsets-only term vectors and the other highlighters are not affected.

Mechanism, terms and numbers584 words

The classic Highlighter with a QueryScorer finds the words to mark by rewriting the query's clauses against a tiny one-field index of the document being highlighted. When the token stream comes from term vectors with positions and offsets, that index is a TermVectorLeafReader, whose only field (shadowed_field) has no norms, doc values or vectors, and since #15678 (10.5.0) its getFieldInfos() returns that field for every name. #15678 also removed the FieldExistsQuery skip that #12088 (9.5.0) had added. So a FieldExistsQuery on any field, or a query that rewrites to one such as LongField.newRangeQuery(f, Long.MIN_VALUE, Long.MAX_VALUE), reaches FieldExistsQuery.rewrite, which throws IllegalStateException naming shadowed_field. The highlight call fails for every hit; 10.4.0 returns the quick brown <B>fox</B>. Search results, re-analysed text and offsets-only term vectors are not affected. Restoring the skip fixes it and passes all 732 tests of the highlighter module with the repro tests added.

Terms

#
  • Classic Highlighter: org.apache.lucene.search.highlight.Highlighter with a QueryScorer. The scorer asks WeightedSpanTermExtractor which terms of the query occur in the token stream of the text.
  • Token stream from term vectors: instead of re-analysing the stored text, TokenSources can rebuild the tokens from the document's term vectors (getTermVectorTokenStreamOrNull, or getTokenStream(field, tvFields, text, analyzer, max), which prefers term vectors when they have offsets). This is the usual speed-up for this highlighter.
  • Internal reader: for queries it has no special case for (apart from spatial queries and multi-term queries it does not expand), the extractor calls query.rewrite(...) on a one-document reader made from the token stream, then extracts terms from the result. With term vectors that have positions and offsets the reader is a TermVectorLeafReader; otherwise the text is indexed into a MemoryIndex. A DelegatingLeafReader wraps it and answers every field name with its one field, shadowed_field.
  • FieldExistsQuery: matches documents that have a value in a field, using norms, doc values or vectors. Its rewrite throws IllegalStateException for a field that exists but indexes none of them.

Exposure and likelihood425 words
  • Who. Users of the classic Highlighter with a QueryScorer who take token streams from term vectors with positions and offsets: TokenSources.getTermVectorTokenStreamOrNull, or TokenSources.getTokenStream(field, tvFields, text, analyzer, max) when the field has such vectors. Neither method is deprecated; TokenSources recommends positions on the term vector, and the package javadoc presents term vectors as the faster way to highlight.
  • Trigger. Any clause of the highlighted query that is, or rewrites to, a FieldExistsQuery, on any field: exists filters on doc-values, norms, vector, keyword or numeric fields and on the highlighted field itself; full-range numeric and unbounded sorted or sorted-set doc-values ranges, and the full-range LongField range; an IndexOrDocValuesQuery whose index side is an exists query (the extractor reads only that side); the same under ConstantScore, Boost, FunctionScore, DisjunctionMax or nested Boolean queries. MUST_NOT clauses are skipped by the extractor and do not trigger it.
  • Scale. The finder's matrix ran 7 document shapes x 3 text queries x about 130 query atoms x 5 occurrences (13,884 searchable queries) and highlighted every hit in 26 modes: 533,104 checks on main. 36 atoms throw this exception, all in the three QueryScorer term-vector modes and none in the UnifiedHighlighter or FastVectorHighlighter modes. (The matrix also records other, unrelated exceptions in some modes; they are not part of this report.) #16582 (10.6) makes ranges that cover every value of a field rewrite to FieldExistsQuery on the real index, but not on the highlighter's internal reader, which has no doc-values skipper: executed on main, such ranges still highlight from term vectors, and only the full Long.MIN_VALUE..Long.MAX_VALUE range throws, as on 10.5.2.
  • Effect. One unchecked IllegalStateException per highlight call, so the request gets no highlights or an error. One document is enough, and no special settings are needed: it fails with the test framework's randomized directory and config (the repro) and with a plain IndexWriterConfig, the default codec and a plain IndexSearcher (the diagram probe); assertions on and off. Nothing is written and search results are correct.
  • Opt-in. Term vectors with positions and offsets are an index-time choice, and the classic Highlighter is the older highlighter (#16820, an open proposal, would deprecate it in 11.0; nothing is deprecated yet). No matching public report was found in the four months since 10.5.0.
  • Workaround. Highlight with a query that has no exists or full-range clauses, or override the protected QueryScorer.newTermExtractor to return a WeightedSpanTermExtractor whose protected isQueryUnsupported also returns true for FieldExistsQuery. Both give the quick brown <B>fox</B> on main from a package outside Lucene, with assertions on and off.

Why it is a bug, not intended

#
  • #12088 (9.5.0) made the highlighter skip FieldExistsQuery to "avoid throwing an exception"; #15678 promises such rewrites "will not throw exceptions during highlighting".
  • #15678 removed the skip, apparently expecting a clean rewrite; that holds only on the MemoryIndex path its tests use.
  • The real price has doc values; the error names an internal field, and 10.4.0 highlights the same hit.
The full argument309 words
  • #12088 (9.5.0), commit fc7b937affe "Don't throw UOE when highlighting FieldExistsQuery": "This commit makes WeightedSpanTermExtractor aware of FieldExistsQuery, so that it can just ignore it and avoid throwing an exception." Its test TestHighlighter.testFieldExistsQuery still exists.
  • #15678 (10.5.0), commit a8b3d1a9481d: "This commit adds a more useful implementation, meaning that queries calling getFieldInfos() during rewrite will not throw exceptions during highlighting." Its PR body: the new FieldInfos "ensures that any current or future query types that rely on index schema metadata during rewrite can be handled gracefully by the highlighter". The issue it fixed, #15668, asked to end the "whack-a-mole" of query optimizations that break the highlighter.
  • Why the removal slipped through (our reading; the thread does not discuss term vectors). On #15678 the reviewer asked "maybe let's remove the special-casing for FieldExistsQuery as well?" and the author did. #15678's two tests and testFieldExistsQuery take the MemoryIndex path, where the internal field has norms: testSortedNumericDocValuesRangeQuery and testFieldExistsQuery use a CannedTokenStream, and TestHighlightCustomQuery.testHighlightQueryRequiringMetadata re-analyses the text with a MockAnalyzer. No upstream test highlights a FieldExistsQuery from term vectors.
  • Correct usage. Public, non-deprecated APIs only; the document matches the query on the real index; the error names shadowed_field, a field the user never created; it fires even for an exists clause on the highlighted field, which has norms.
  • The class javadoc leaves queries it does not support to subclasses: they may override extract and extractUnknownQuery "to support additional, by default unsupported queries", and the default extractUnknownQuery, reached when a rewrite returns the query unchanged, does nothing. It does not promise that a rewrite cannot throw, so it is weaker support than the two commit messages.
  • Stability markers, both ways. Highlighter, QueryScorer, WeightedSpanTermExtractor and the two TokenSources methods are public and not deprecated; TermVectorLeafReader and the FieldInfo constructor are @lucene.experimental, and TokenStreamFromTermVector is @lucene.internal.
The devil's-advocate check176 words

Strongest case (a reviewer from another model family, OpenAI GPT-5.6, and an intended-behaviour checker): the classic Highlighter is a lexical term extractor that never promised to handle every query; FieldExistsQuery has no terms to highlight, and its exception is a deliberate fail-fast for a field without norms, doc values or vectors; the term-vector reader is a deliberately partial @lucene.experimental view; #15678 removed the special case on purpose; the #12088 CHANGES line names only UnsupportedOperationException; and #16820 proposes deprecating the classic Highlighter.

Why it fails: the fail-fast fires on the highlighter's own description of the schema, not on the caller's field (the real price has doc values); both commit messages state a no-exception intent for exactly this kind of rewrite; the removal was justified only for the path its tests covered; 10.4.0 behaves differently with the same code and query; and a proposed deprecation is not a decision. Both reviewers rated the argument weak. When filing, base the contract on the two commit messages, the #12088 test and the 10.4.0-to-10.5.0 change, not on the CHANGES line.

B. Let TermVectorLeafReader report norms (validated by the checks)

#

Not a fix: user-side workarounds (validated by the checks)

#

Until a fix ships: highlight with a query that has no exists or full-range clauses, or override the protected QueryScorer.newTermExtractor to return a WeightedSpanTermExtractor whose protected isQueryUnsupported also returns true for FieldExistsQuery.

new QueryScorer(query, "body") {
  @Override
  protected WeightedSpanTermExtractor newTermExtractor(String defaultField) {
    return new WeightedSpanTermExtractor(defaultField) {
      @Override
      protected boolean isQueryUnsupported(Class<? extends Query> clazz) {
        return clazz == FieldExistsQuery.class || super.isQueryUnsupported(clazz);
      }
    };
  }
}

Trade-offs

Application code only; it does not fix Lucene, and every application on the term-vector path needs it.

Downstream note

By code reading only, Solr's original highlighter gets term-vector token streams through the same TokenSources method; Solr is still on Lucene 10.4.0, so it is not affected yet.

How it was found

#

Code reading, not a formal method: reading #15678's internal-reader change, a planning step wrote a small probe (term-vector highlighting plus an exists filter) that threw on main, and bisected it to 10.5.0. A hunt reduced it to a public-API JUnit and widened it with a 533,104-check matrix of query types and highlighter modes.

The method in full180 words
  • First signal (non-formal). The hunt's planning analysis read the DelegatingLeafReader remap that #15678 added, prompted by downstream reports of its predecessor UnsupportedOperationException, and wrote a small probe: a term-vector field plus a FieldExistsQuery filter. It threw on main and was bisected to 10.5.0 by release.
  • Reduction. A hunt unit on the classic and fast-vector highlighters turned it into the public-API JUnit of the repro and ran it on main and the release jars.
  • Widening (bounded-exhaustive checking of the stock jars). A trigger model enumerated 11 token-stream cells (term-vector flag combinations, non-empty and empty text, the analysis path with and without caching and payloads) and checked "highlighting throws iff the internal reader is a TermVectorLeafReader" within them (holds, 0 violations). A matrix ran 7 document shapes x 3 text queries x about 130 query atoms x 5 occurrences and highlighted every hit in 26 modes: 533,104 checks on main (455,052, with a smaller atom set, on 10.5.2 and 10.4.0), grouped by throwing frame: 36 atoms throw this exception on main, only in the classic Highlighter's term-vector modes; 10.4.0 has none.

Bug record

LFV-824, snapshot of 2026-10-29

Registry title
Classic Highlighter throws IllegalStateException for any query that contains a FieldExistsQuery (or a query that rewrites to one, such as an open-ended LongField range) when the token stream comes from term vectors with positions and offsets (regression in 10.5.0 from GITHUB#15678)
Area
highlighter / classic Highlighter (QueryScorer, WeightedSpanTermExtractor term-vector path)
Priority
Medium, rank 275: A released 10.5.0 regression where the classic Highlighter throws on every hit when term vectors are the source and the query contains an ordinary FieldExistsQuery or an open-ended range; the failure is loud and limited to the legacy term-vector path.
Impact class
crash
Impact note
Applications that highlight with the classic Highlighter and a QueryScorer from term vectors stored with positions and offsets (TokenSources.getTermVectorTokenStreamOrNull, or TokenSources.getTokenStream when the field has such vectors) get an IllegalStateException instead of highlights for every hit on 10.5.0-10.5.2, branch_10_6 (10.6.0) and main whenever the highlighted query contains a FieldExistsQuery on any field (in a MUST, FILTER, SHOULD or nested clause; MUST_NOT clauses are skipped) or a query that rewrites to one (LongField.newRangeQuery(f, Long.MIN_VALUE, Long.MAX_VALUE), full-range or open-ended doc-values slow ranges, IndexOrDocValuesQuery with a FieldExistsQuery side, and FieldExistsQuery under ConstantScore, Boost, FunctionScore or DisjunctionMax); default IndexWriterConfig, codec and searcher, -ea and -da, one document suffices; re-analysed token streams and offsets-only term vectors are not affected, search results are correct, and nothing is written to the index.
Summary
You store a text field with term vectors (positions and offsets), search with a query such as +body:fox plus a FieldExistsQuery filter on another field, and highlight the hit with the classic Highlighter from its term vectors; you expect 'the quick brown <B>fox</B>', which 10.4.0 and earlier return. Since 10.5.0 the call throws IllegalStateException 'FieldExistsQuery requires that the field indexes doc values, norms or vectors, but field 'shadowed_field' exists and indexes neither of these data structures', naming an internal field the user never created. The highlighter rewrites the query against its own one-field reader built from the term vectors, whose field info has no norms or doc values, and since GITHUB#15678 it no longer skips FieldExistsQuery as GITHUB#12088 had made it do.
