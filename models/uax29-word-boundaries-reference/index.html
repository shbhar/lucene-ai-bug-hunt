Bugs found

  • StandardTokenizer (StandardAnalyzer's tokenizer) and UAX29URLEmailTokenizer misapply the UAX #29 Hebrew quote rules WB7a-WB7c next to other word characters: a Hebrew letter + ASCII apostrophe swallows a following digit or underscore (BET'2 is one term), an ASCII apostrophe after a Hebrew letter that ends a mid-word chain is dropped (HE.SHIN' -> HE.SHIN), and a word with an ASCII quotation mark between Hebrew letters is split when it also contains a mid-word '.', ':' or quote (ALEF.TSADI"LAMED, TAV"ALEF.BET) (bug LFV-734; repro · bug page)
