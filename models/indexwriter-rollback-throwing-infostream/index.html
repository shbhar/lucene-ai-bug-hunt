Findings

  • If IndexWriter's InfoStream throws at the first log message of the rollback step ('rollback'), close() or rollback() throws and the writer is left closing, not closed: the write lock stays held and a later close() or rollback() waits until its thread is interrupted (run through rollback() on 25 releases from 2.9.4 to 10.6.0, on branch_11x and on main, and through close() on 10 of them from 4.10.0 on and on both branches; before 4.7.1 only a later close() waits; after a rollback() that threw while a prepareCommit() was pending, a later close() throws IllegalStateException instead of waiting when commit on close is on (the default; run on 10.6.0, branch_11x and main); the interrupt was run on 9.1.0, 10.6.0, branch_11x and main; needs an InfoStream that throws at that message) (finding LFV-1543; repro · finding page)
