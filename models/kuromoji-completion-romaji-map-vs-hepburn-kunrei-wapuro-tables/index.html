Bugs found

  • Japanese completion suggester (JapaneseCompletionAnalyzer / JapaneseCompletionFilter): words with a small tsu before shi, chi or tsu (雑誌, 決して, マッチ, 三つ) are never suggested for their Hepburn or input-method spellings zasshi, kesshite, macchi/matchi, mittsu, because romaji_map.txt maps ッシ only to 'ssi', ッチ only to 'tti' and ッツ only to 'ttu', while the base rows シ/チ/ツ list shi/chi/tsu and the neighbouring rows ッシャ and ッチャ do double the consonant ('ssha', 'ccha') (bug LFV-849; repro · bug page)
  • Kuromoji's completion table romaji_map.txt has no rows for チェ, イェ, ツァ/ツィ/ツェ/ツォ, ディ/デュ and ウォ, although it has rows for their siblings (チャ/チュ/チョ, シェ/ジェ, ッイェ, ティ/テュ, ウィ/ウェ), so raw-romaji prefixes such as 'che', 'ye' and 'tsa' suggest nothing for words like チェック, イェール and ツァー (bug LFV-850; repro · bug page)
  • Kuromoji's completion table romaji_map.txt has three typo rows: the ッヘ row uses a hiragana へ (U+3078) in its key, so katakana ッヘ is romanized 'xtuhe' instead of 'hhe' and a word like ヨッヘン is not suggested for 'yohhen', and the ッスォ and ッドゥ rows carry the copied spellings 'suxo' and 'ddoxi' (bug LFV-848; repro · bug page)
  • Kuromoji completion suggester (JapaneseCompletionAnalyzer / JapaneseCompletionFilter, QUERY mode) suggests nothing while an input method shows a small tsu plus a pending c, j, f or v ('まっｃ' while typing maccha for 抹茶, 'ばっｊ' for bajji, 'すたっｆ' for sutaffu), because romaji_map.txt has the partial-input rows ッk..ッw but none for ッc, ッj, ッf or ッv, although its full rows double those letters (ッチャ ccha, ッジ jji, ッフ ffu, ッヴ vvu) (bug LFV-854; repro · bug page)
