# Quantized MIP score of a document equal to its segment centroid is 3.0 instead of 3.5

Bug LFV-079 · Area: codecs / vectors · Priority: low · Impact: silent wrong results · Novelty: known upstream

AI-assisted finding, confirmed by automated checks, not yet triaged by a human Lucene committer. This site files nothing upstream.

## What it shows

With the OSQ format and MAXIMUM_INNER_PRODUCT, you score a document whose vector equals its segment's centroid. You expect the exact inner-product score (3.5 in the repro). Instead float32 absorption in the corrective tail gives 3.0.

## Expected vs actual

- Expected: You expect the exact inner-product score (3.5 in the repro).
- Actual: Float32 absorption in the corrective tail gives 3.0.
