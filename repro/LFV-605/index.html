AI-assisted bug hunt in Apache Lucene

Kuromoji's romaji reading (ToStringUtil.getRomanization, used by JapaneseReadingFormFilter with useRomaji=true) writes the small tsu ッ as a doubled consonant only before the k, s, t and p rows and silently drops it before any other kana, so loanwords with ッ before g, z/j, d, b or h/f lose it: バッグ 'bag' and バグ 'bug' both become 'bagu', ベッド becomes 'bedo'

What it shows

You analyze Japanese text with JapaneseTokenizer and JapaneseReadingFormFilter(useRomaji=true), whose romanizer is documented as 'Romanize katakana with modified hepburn', and expect the small tsu ッ to double the next consonant, as the method already does for キット -> 'kitto' and マッチ -> 'matchi'. Instead the 'ッ' case appends a consonant only for the カ, サ, タ and パ rows and appends nothing otherwise, so バッグ 'bag' becomes 'bagu', the same term as バグ 'bug', ベッド becomes 'bedo' and スタッフ becomes 'sutafu'. Distinct words then match each other, and a romaji query 'baggu' finds no document that contains バッグ.

Expected vs actual

  • Expected: バッグ → baggu (not バグ's bagu), ベッド → beddo, ビッグ → biggu: the ッ doubles the next consonant, as in キット → kitto.
  • Actual: 7 of 7 lose the ッ (バッグ → bagu, ベッド → bedo, ビッグ → bigu, キッズ → kizu,...), and the filter emits bagu for バッグ.
