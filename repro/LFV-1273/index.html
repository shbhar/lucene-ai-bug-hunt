AI-assisted bug hunt in Apache Lucene

CheckIndex.checkImpacts' cross-level scan advances the higher level's cursor once per undominated lower-level impact and never re-tests that impact, so level 1 [(1,1),(2,2)] over level 0 [(1,1),(5,5)] passes CheckIndex.testPostings although no level-1 impact dominates (5,5), while level 1 [(1,1)] alone is rejected (since 8.0.0)

What it shows

You index two documents in field f ('a' and 'a a a a a', so term a has (freq, norm) (1,1) and (5,5)) and run CheckIndex.testPostings at the slow-check level over a reader whose impacts for a report level 0 [(1,1),(5,5)] and level 1 [(1,1),(2,2)], both valid to the end of the postings, as a broken custom postings format could. You expect the error that CheckIndex's own comment and message describe, 'Found impact (5,5) on level 0 but no impact on level 1 triggers a better score', because no level-1 impact has freq >= 5 and level 1 therefore under-states document 1. Instead status.error is null on main (-ea and -da) and on 10.6.0, 10.5.2 and 9.12.3 (-da): after (5,5) misses (1,1) the scan moves the level-1 cursor to (2,2) and goes on to the next level-0 impact (there is none), so (5,5) is never compared with (2,2), while level 1 [(1,1)] alone gets the expected error.

Expected vs actual

  • Expected: checkImpacts throws CheckIndexException: "Found impact (5,5) on level 0 but no impact on level 1 triggers a better score: freqs=[1, 2] norms=[1, 2]", as it does with the fix.
  • Actual: checkImpacts returns normally, so the test fails with "Expected exception CheckIndexException but no exception was thrown", with assertions on and off.
