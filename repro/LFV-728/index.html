AI-assisted bug hunt in Apache Lucene

SmartChineseAnalyzer indexes whitespace characters as terms: VERTICAL TAB, FORM FEED, OGHAM SPACE MARK and the eleven spaces U+2000-U+200A (EN QUAD to HAIR SPACE, among them EN, EM and THIN SPACE) each become a one-character term, because Utility.getCharType classifies them OTHER instead of SPACE_LIKE

Bug LFV-728Area: analysis-smartcn / HHMM segmenter character classes (Utility.getCharType SPACE_LIKE test -> HHMMSegmenter OTHER branch -> one-character STRING term; SmartChineseAnalyzer and HMMChineseTokenizer)Priority: mediumImpact: silent wrong resultsNovelty: known upstream

AI-assisted finding, confirmed by automated checks, not yet triaged by a human Lucene committer. This site files nothing upstream.

What it shows

You index text with the stock SmartChineseAnalyzer, whose segmenter has a character class for 'Characters that act as a space' (CharType.SPACE_LIKE) and skips those characters, so you expect any space to separate words without a trace, as an ordinary space, a tab or the ideographic space U+3000 does. Instead Utility.getCharType knows only those five, and VERTICAL TAB, FORM FEED, OGHAM SPACE MARK and U+2000-U+200A fall through to OTHER and are each indexed as a one-character term: 'hello<THIN SPACE>world' gives [hello, U+2009, world], a TermQuery for U+2009 alone finds the document, and '中<U+2009>国' gives [中, U+2009, 国] where '中 国' gives [中国]. So the exact phrase "hello world" matches only 3 of 7 documents that differ only in the separator (space, tab and U+3000 match; FORM FEED, U+1680, EM SPACE and THIN SPACE miss), and HTML indented with &emsp;&emsp; gets two whitespace terms, with no error.

Expected vs actual

  • Expected: You expect any space to separate words without a trace, as an ordinary space, a tab or the ideographic space U+3000 does.
  • Actual: Utility.getCharType knows only those five, and VERTICAL TAB, FORM FEED, OGHAM SPACE MARK and U+2000-U+200A fall through to OTHER and are each indexed as a one-character term: 'hello<THIN SPACE>world' gives [hello, U+2009, world], a TermQuery for U+2009 alone finds the document, and '中<U+2009>国' gives [中, U+2009, 国] where '中 国' gives [中国].

  • How to read the outcome: a failing test is the bug reproducing (the failure message states expected against actual).
  • Seeds: a rerun with the same seed replays the same random choices. Assertions off (-da -dsa -Dtests.asserts=false) is the production-like setting.
# Lucene checkout at the tested commit, built with: ./gradlew --no-daemon assemble   (JDK 25)
LUCENE=/path/to/lucene; V=11.0.0-SNAPSHOT
CP=$LUCENE/lucene/core/build/libs/lucene-core-$V.jar
